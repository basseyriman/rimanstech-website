# RimansTech Brand Assets

Official logo files for print, digital and document use.

## Files

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| `rimanstech-wordmark-dark.svg` | Letterheads, business cards, light backgrounds, print |
| `rimanstech-wordmark-light.svg` | Dark backgrounds, presentations, website footer |
| `rimanstech-monogram.svg` | App icon, favicon, social avatars, small digital |
| `rimanstech-wordmark-dark.png` | Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, email signatures |
| `rimanstech-monogram.png` | App stores, high-resolution digital icon |

## Wordmark structure

```
RIMANS          |  INDUSTRIES
TECH
```

- **RIMANS** — Carbon Black `#101110`
- **TECH** — Deep Forest `#183C32`
- **INDUSTRIES** — Graphite `#50534F`

## Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the letter "T" in TECH on all sides.

## Minimum sizes

- Wordmark: 120px wide (digital), 30mm wide (print)
- Monogram: 32px (digital), 8mm (print)

## Do not

- Stretch or distort proportions
- Change brand colours
- Add effects, shadows or gradients
- Place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast

## Letterhead placement

Top-left or centred, 40–50mm wide on A4 ivory/off-white paper (`#F5F3EE`).
